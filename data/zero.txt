In reviewing governance records for Northbridge Risk Holdings (“NRH”), counsel noted that Elena Voss serves as ceo_of NRH and also acts as director_of Harbor Re Analytics (“HRA”). Corporate files confirm that NRH owns HRA, and HRA remains subsidiary_of NRH. During diligence, both NRH and HRA were identified as party_to_agreement under the Capital Transfer and Services Agreement (“CTSA”). Execution pages further show Elena Voss as signatory_for the CTSA. Separate compliance materials indicate that NRH is regulated_by the Federal Insurance Compliance Bureau (“FICB”). In addition, HRA is investigated_by FICB regarding reporting controls, although no finding has been issued. Ancillary correspondence discussed office renovations, archived underwriting manuals, and a delayed software migration. Several witnesses used abbreviations interchangeably, referring to Northbridge Risk Holdings as NRH, Harbor Re Analytics as HRA, the Capital Transfer and Services Agreement as CTSA, and the Federal Insurance Compliance Bureau as FICB.
