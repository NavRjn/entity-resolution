Northbridge Risk Holdings (NRH) controls Harbor Re Analytics (HRA).
Elena Voss is the CEO of Northbridge Risk Holdings and a director of Harbor Re Analytics.

Both NRH and HRA participate in the Capital Transfer and Services Agreement (CTSA).
Elena Voss is a signatory to the CTSA.

The Federal Insurance Compliance Bureau (FICB) has regulatory oversight relationships with Northbridge Risk Holdings and Harbor Re Analytics.
