AURORA STRATEGIC HOLDINGS, INC.
Combined Compliance Integration Memorandum and Transitional Services Summary
Prepared for the Audit Committee of Aurora Strategic Holdings, Inc. ("Aurora")
Date: March 14, 2025
--------------------

This Combined Compliance Integration Memorandum (the "Memorandum") summarizes material operational, regulatory, governance, and transactional matters arising from the acquisition by Aurora Strategic Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation, of substantially all equity interests in Helios BioAnalytics Corporation, formerly known as HBA Diagnostics Holdings LLC ("Helios"), pursuant to the Equity Purchase Agreement dated January 9, 2025 (the "Equity Purchase Agreement").

The transaction was negotiated among Aurora Strategic Holdings, Inc.; Redwood Capital Partners IV, L.P.; Helios BioAnalytics Corporation; and certain legacy shareholders identified on Schedule A thereto, including Dr. Emily Carrow, Michael J. Levin, and Sandrine Moreau.

SECTION 2 — BACKGROUND OF THE TRANSACTION

On January 9, 2025, Aurora entered into the Equity Purchase Agreement with Redwood Capital Partners IV, L.P. and the selling shareholders of Helios BioAnalytics Corporation. The aggregate consideration consisted of (a) cash in the amount of USD 412,500,000, (b) contingent earn-out payments not to exceed USD 85,000,000, and (c) assumption of specified liabilities under the Transitional Manufacturing and Support Agreement ("TMSA").

The Equity Purchase Agreement contains customary representations and warranties relating to:
• export controls;
• anti-bribery compliance;
• data privacy matters;
• tax liabilities;
• employee benefit plans;
• environmental liabilities;
• intellectual property ownership.

Section 4.12(b) of the Equity Purchase Agreement references a prior internal investigation involving unauthorized transfers of genomic testing data between servers maintained by BlueHarbor Analytics Pte. Ltd. in Singapore and subcontracted processing nodes operated by Norwalk Biometric Solutions, Inc.

During confirmatory diligence, Sterling & Vale LLP identified deficiencies in retention schedules maintained by Aurora Clinical Systems LLC. A memorandum prepared by Priya Ramanathan dated December 11, 2024 noted:

```
"Certain records associated with the AegisPoint(TM) oncology workflow product
appear to have been archived inconsistently across U.S. and E.U. instances."
```

SECTION 3 — REGULATORY AND COMPLIANCE MATTERS

3.1 FDA Correspondence

On February 17, 2025, the U.S. Food and Drug Administration issued a notice of inspectional observations concerning the Helios Austin laboratory facility. The notice referenced procedural deficiencies in calibration logs associated with the NovaSeq Vector platform.

The FDA communication also identified incomplete chain-of-custody documentation for clinical batches shipped to facilities in Rotterdam, Netherlands and Dublin, Ireland.

An OCR-scanned excerpt attached to the inspection file appears below:

```
"calib ration logs were not signed by auth orized supervisor pers onnel
 for testing cycle 24-B;
 sample stor age tempe ratures from Jan. 11 thru Jan. 14 were not
 fully reconciled."
```

3.2 SEC and Financial Reporting

Aurora filed a Current Report on Form 8-K with the Securities and Exchange Commission on January 13, 2025 announcing completion of the acquisition. The filing incorporated unaudited financial statements prepared by Grant Mercer Advisory Group.

The Finance Department identified certain discrepancies between:
• management reporting systems;
• local statutory ledgers maintained in Ontario, Canada;
• consolidated ERP extracts generated from SAP Horizon Enterprise Suite.

A remediation initiative was launched under the oversight of Charles E. Whitaker III and Elena Kovacs.

3.3 Data Privacy and Cybersecurity

Northbridge Data Security Ltd. completed a penetration assessment dated February 28, 2025 concerning legacy Helios infrastructure. The assessment identified elevated risks associated with unsupported remote-access appliances located in Warsaw, Poland and Prague, Czech Republic.

The report further noted that:
"legacy VPN certificates issued to HBA Dx contractors remained active after
termination dates."

Aurora CS Europe GmbH initiated notice procedures under Article 33 of the General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") after discovery of a credential exposure event involving approximately 12,400 patient records.

SECTION 4 — MATERIAL AGREEMENTS

4.1 Transitional Manufacturing and Support Agreement

Pursuant to the Transitional Manufacturing and Support Agreement, Meridian Specialty Pharma, Inc. agreed to continue supplying reagent compounds for use in the NovaSeq Vector product line through June 30, 2026.

Pricing schedules under the TMSA include volume adjustments tied to:
• annual throughput;
• reagent scarcity indexes;
• transportation surcharges;
• force majeure events.

4.2 Cloud Services and Infrastructure Agreements

BlueHarbor Analytics Pte. Ltd. entered into the Master Cloud Processing Agreement with Atlas Vertex Computing AG, headquartered in Zurich, Switzerland.

The Master Cloud Processing Agreement permits cross-border replication of encrypted patient datasets subject to:
(a) pseudonymization requirements;
(b) regional encryption key segregation;
(c) quarterly audit rights.

An amendment executed on February 3, 2025 references migration of selected workloads from "AVC Legacy Cluster 7" to "Atlas Vertex Sovereign Node-EU2."

4.3 Insurance and Risk Allocation

Eastgate Commercial Insurance Agency arranged a layered cyber-liability program involving:
- Harbor Mutual Indemnity plc
- Crescent Continental Re Ltd.
- Vantage Harbor Reinsurance SPC

The policy aggregate limit equals USD 150 million, subject to exclusions for:
• nation-state cyberwarfare;
• pre-existing known incidents;
• unencrypted removable media losses.

SECTION 5 — SUBSIDIARY OPERATIONS

5.1 European Operations

Aurora CS Europe GmbH maintains branch offices in Frankfurt, Germany and Milan, Italy. Sandrine Moreau reported that certain vendor onboarding files lacked anti-money laundering certifications required under revised E.U. procurement directives.

The internal audit team also identified inconsistent invoice references among:
- Aurora CS Europe GmbH
- MedAxis Procurement Solutions BV
- Baltic Laboratory Freight OU

One invoice excerpt contained the following formatting issue:

```
INV-4471-A / recvd by "Aurora C S Eur ope GmbH"
```

5.2 Canadian Manufacturing

Meridian Specialty Pharma Canada ULC operates a sterile manufacturing facility in Mississauga, Ontario. Ontario Health Compliance Directorate inspectors conducted a site review on January 29, 2025.

The inspectors requested:
• revised contamination control protocols;
• additional lot traceability documentation;
• environmental monitoring validation reports.

5.3 Asia-Pacific Activities

BlueHarbor Analytics Pte. Ltd. maintains regional support centers in Singapore and Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. Certain software-development contractors were retained through Pacific Rim Technical Staffing Co.

An internal email dated February 8, 2025 stated:

```
"Several contractors assigned to the Helios migration project continue to use
historical entity references including 'Aurora Strategic Group' and 'HBA
Diagnostics Holdings'."
```

SECTION 6 — GOVERNANCE MATTERS

6.1 Audit Committee Findings

The Audit Committee of Aurora Strategic Holdings, Inc. met on March 2, 2025 in New York, New York. Present were Jonathan R. Keene, Charles E. Whitaker III, Priya Ramanathan, Elena Kovacs, and outside counsel from Sterling & Vale LLP.

Minutes reflect discussion regarding:
• whistleblower hotline activity;
• vendor diligence gaps;
• delayed reconciliation controls;
• cybersecurity insurance recoverability.

A draft resolution proposed engaging Beacon Ridge Advisory Services LLC to conduct an enterprise-wide controls assessment.

6.2 Historical Compliance Issues

Prior to the acquisition, Helios BioAnalytics Corporation received an administrative inquiry from the Financial Conduct Authority relating to securities communications distributed to investors in London, England.

The inquiry referenced investor materials prepared by Redwood Capital Partners IV, L.P. during 2023 fundraising activities.

Additionally, the International Diagnostics Consortium issued a warning letter concerning use of unapproved claims in promotional literature associated with the AegisPoint oncology workflow.

6.3 Intellectual Property Portfolio

Aurora and Helios jointly maintain intellectual property associated with:
• AegisPoint(TM)
• NovaSeq Vector
• SentinelGrid Analytics
• OmniPath Clinical Exchange

Trademark registrations are held through various entities, including Meridian Specialty Pharma, Inc. and Aurora Clinical Systems LLC.

SECTION 7 — EMPLOYMENT AND BENEFITS

As part of the acquisition integration process, selected Helios personnel entered into Retention and Transition Bonus Agreements.

The Human Resources Department identified inconsistent payroll coding references among:

7.2 Pension and Deferred Compensation Matters

Hudson National Trust Company acts as trustee for the Aurora Executive Deferred Compensation Plan.

Commonwealth Fidelity Bank provides custodial support for certain offshore reserve accounts associated with Vantage Harbor Reinsurance SPC.

SECTION 8 — LITIGATION AND DISPUTES

8.1 Pending Litigation

On December 4, 2024, Norwalk Biometric Solutions, Inc. filed a complaint in the United States District Court for the Southern District of New York alleging breach of contract and negligent misrepresentation.

The complaint alleges that Helios BioAnalytics Corporation improperly terminated data-processing services prior to expiration of the Data Infrastructure Services Agreement dated May 18, 2022.

Damages sought exceed USD 18,700,000.

8.2 Employment Claims

A former contractor based in Prague, Czech Republic asserted claims relating to unpaid consulting fees allegedly owed by Aurora CS Europe GmbH.

Counsel from Sterling & Vale LLP believes the claim lacks substantial merit but recommended establishment of a limited reserve.

8.3 Regulatory Exposure

The European Medicines Agency requested supplemental pharmacovigilance documentation relating to Meridian Specialty Pharma, Inc. products distributed within the European Economic Area.

No formal enforcement proceeding has been initiated as of the date of this Memorandum.
