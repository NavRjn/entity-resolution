Northbridge Risk Holdings (NRH) owns Harbor Re Analytics (HRA).
Elena Voss is the CEO of Northbridge Risk Holdings and director of Harbor Re Analytics.

Both NRH and HRA are party to the Capital Transfer and Services Agreement (CTSA).
Elena Voss is a signatory for the CTSA.

Northbridge Risk Holdings is regulated by the Federal Insurance Compliance Bureau (FICB).
Harbor Re Analytics is investigated by the Federal Insurance Compliance Bureau (FICB).
